📊 R/FANTASY TOP NOVELS (PART 3)

Part 1 Part 2 Part 3
Catalog Section: Rankings 17 to 24.

17. Unsouled

By Will Wight • Series: Cradle

In a mystical clan society where martial artists climb levels of power, Lindon is branded 'Unsouled'—condemned to a life without magic or status until he discovers a path of relentless perseverance.

18. Tigana

By Guy Gavriel Kay • Standalone Masterpiece

The peninsula of the Palm has been conquered by two rival sorcerer-tyrants. Even worse, the memory of its true name, Tigana, has been magically erased from the minds of its people.

19. The Emperor's Soul

By Brandon Sanderson • Hugo Award-winning Novella

Forger Shai is caught and given a single chance to save her life: forge a new soul for the Emperor who was left brain-dead after an assassination attempt.

20. Piranesi

By Susanna Clarke • Award-winning Fantasy

Piranesi's house is no ordinary building: its rooms are infinite, its corridors endless, and an ocean is trapped inside its lower halls, hiding profound mysteries.

21. The Ocean at the End of the Lane

By Neil Gaiman • Modern Dark Fantasy

A middle-aged man returns to his childhood home and remembers the terrifying supernatural events of his seventh year and the strange family living down the lane.

22. The Traitor Baru Cormorant

By Seth Dickinson • Series: The Masquerade

Baru Cormorant's homeland is colonized by the sprawling Empire of Masks. She resolves to gain power from within the imperial bureaucracy to destroy it from the inside.

23. The City of Brass

By S.A. Chakraborty • Series: The Daevabad Trilogy

A young con artist in 18th-century Cairo accidentally summons a brooding, mysterious djinn warrior, pulling her into a legendary magical city full of political intrigue.

24. The Goblin Emperor

By Katherine Addison • Honorable Mention

A concluding favorite focusing on compassion, integrity, and quiet strength inside an overwhelming imperial throne room.
