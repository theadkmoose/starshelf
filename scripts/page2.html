📊 R/FANTASY TOP NOVELS (PART 2)

Part 1 Part 2 Part 3
Catalog Section: Rankings 9 to 16.

9. Gardens of the Moon

By Steven Erikson • Series: Malazan Book of the Fallen

The Malazan Empire simmers with internal conflict, blood-soaked campaigns, and divine intervention behind the scenes, weaving an intricate tapestry of ancient gods, ascendants, and mortal soldiers.

10. The Priory of the Orange Tree

By Samantha Shannon • Standalone Epic Fantasy

A divided world, an unyielding dragon threat, and a secret society of women sworn to protect humanity from ancient evils that threaten to shatter international stability.

11. The Goblin Emperor

By Katherine Addison • Standalone Steampunk Fantasy

The youngest, half-goblin son of the Emperor unexpectedly inherits an intricate, backstabbing court after an airship crash kills the rest of the royal family, navigating treacherous political waters with grace.

12. The Fifth Season

By N.K. Jemisin • Series: The Broken Earth

This is the way the world ends... for the last time. Set in a punishing planet plagued by catastrophic climate shifts called Seasons, where people with earth-shattering magical abilities are oppressed.

13. Red Rising

By Pierce Brown • Series: Red Rising Saga

Darrow is a Red, a lowest-caste helium-miner on a terraformed Mars, discovering that the surface world has long been built on a lie by a ruling color-coded elite.

14. The Shadow of the Torturer

By Gene Wolfe • Series: The Book of the New Sun

Set on a dying Earth under a sputtering red sun, following Severian, an apprentice in the Guild of Torturers who is exiled for showing mercy to a prisoner.

15. The Dragon Reborn

By Robert Jordan • Series: The Wheel of Time

The struggle intensifies as the prophecies of the Dragon Reborn shake the nations to their core, and Rand al'Thor confronts his terrifying destiny.

16. Assassin's Apprentice

By Robin Hobb • Series: Realm of the Elderlings (Farseer)

Young Fitz is the royal bastard son of a prince, raised in the shadows of Buckkeep and trained in the delicate, lethal arts of the royal assassin.
